Lyric Placement
"The night is going still going"
"The night is calling"

Typewriter animation. Letters appear one by one in sync with the outro vocal. Not all at once. The timing follows the singing — never ahead, never behind.

Logo Reveal

Lets Events logo fades in after the final lyric. Small. Centered. Understated. Not a headline — an answer. The viewer discovers the brand, not the other way around.

What Doesn't Appear

No tagline. No CTA. No website URL. No "Follow us". No "Book now". If any of these appear, the video has failed its brief at the last moment.

Color

Pure black. White text at 75% opacity. Logo at 50% opacity, then fades up slightly. The restraint of the end card earns the emotional investment of the first 56 seconds.

SFX 🎵 Music fades naturally

No sound design added. Let the song end. Let silence hold before and after the logo appears.

Critical Success Rules
Every UI element must look immediately like a real phone interface. Not designed. Real.
Lyric text in UI must appear word by word, never ahead of the vocal.
"LETS GO" must land on the hardest beat hit in the chorus.
Smash cut to footage on the exact same frame the beat drops.
No subtitles. Lyrics are delivered through UI only.
No CTA, no dates, no prices anywhere in the video.
No stock footage. Only Lets Events-supplied material in second half.
